Install on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Grid Down from the home screen once while online, and open both tools, so everything is saved to the phone.
  4. In Medical, go to Drug lookup and tap Download all FDA labels on Wi-Fi.

Keep it current

Open the app on Wi-Fi now and then; updates install automatically. Everything you enter (weights, dose logs, checklists) stays on this device only.

Reference only. When any doctor, pharmacist, or emergency service is reachable, use them first.
